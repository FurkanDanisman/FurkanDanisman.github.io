Me: Researcher. I have worked on economics, healthcare, space engineering, theoretical statistics, actuarial science, soccer analytics, and more. I enjoyed exploring each of these domains, but fair to say I still know very little about them.

I am currently doing a PhD in Statistics at the University of Toronto, co-supervised by [Dehan Kong](https://www.statistics.utoronto.ca/people/directories/all-faculty/dehan-kong) (Statistics) and [Zhijing Jin](https://zhijing-jin.com/) (Computer Science). I am also a Graduate Researcher at the [Vector Institute for Artificial Intelligence](https://vectorinstitute.ai/).

Before this, I was a Research Assistant at the [Bank of Canada](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/), where I worked on DeFi lending, LLM-based payment system simulations, and financial access policy. I also developed [DensOLog](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ovLhwwwAAAAJ&hl=en), a non-parametric density estimation algorithm, during my time as a Research Associate at York University.

Lately, I've been drawn to the intersection of large language models (LLMs) and causality. My ultimate goal in life is to be friends with a gorilla.
